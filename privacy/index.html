Who to contact.

AirBridge is developed by Shubham Gopale. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact shubhindia1997@gmail.com. This policy covers the Android app (io.airbridge.android), its Mac companion, and airbridge.work.

This policy describes the data used by the features below. Available features depend on your app version and device. Optional features such as clipboard sync, notification forwarding, and camera sharing are controlled separately.

A direct connection.

AirBridge pairs your Mac and Android phone for communication over your local network. It does not require an account or relay your files through an AirBridge cloud service. Pairing establishes a pinned TLS connection; the QR code carries the identity information used to verify your Mac.

The apps have no advertising or analytics SDK and do not send your files, clipboard, typed text, or notification contents to the developer. Local network discovery uses your device's network address and receiver identity to find the paired Mac. Your device names and pairing identifiers are exchanged between the devices. Scanning a pairing QR code uses your phone's camera locally, without saving the scan or sending the camera feed to the developer. Separately approved live camera sharing is described below.

Your Mac keeps its receiver identity and paired-device records locally, storing device-token hashes rather than the original tokens. Android encrypts its pairing credential using Android Keystore. Removing the phone on Mac revokes its access. Forgetting a Mac only on Android does not revoke the Mac's existing phone record.

Files stay where you send them.

Accepted files are saved on the receiving device. Mac-to-Android sending temporarily stages files in private Mac storage. Completion, cancellation, expiry, and normal shutdown clean up those temporary copies. A crash can leave partial or staged files that require later cleanup.

On Android 10 or later, received files in Downloads survive uninstalling AirBridge. Android 8/9 uses app-private storage, which is removed on uninstall. Android's recent-file list retains up to fifty file links; Mac transfer history is in memory. Files you send, download, open, or share with other apps can be retained by those apps.

File access is limited to files you select or share to AirBridge and files received from your paired Mac. If you choose to open a received APK, AirBridge may request Android's permission to install apps from this source, then hand that file to Android's installer. You must separately confirm installation. AirBridge does not install packages silently or use this feature to update itself automatically.

Clipboard sync and recent copies.

Clipboard sync is opt-in and sends copied text or supported images between your paired devices. The sync service buffers only the latest update temporarily; disconnected copies are not replayed later. Mac image conversion stays in memory. Sync does not save text history to disk.

Separately, when Android allows clipboard access, the AirBridge keyboard can keep up to ten recent copied text items in memory for its clipboard panel. This can include clipboard changes delivered while the keyboard is hidden. These items are replaced as newer copies arrive and cleared when the keyboard service ends. They are not written to disk or sent to the developer. Keeping recent copies does not itself send them to your Mac; automatic sharing requires separate consent.

Android temporarily caches a received clipboard image behind a read-only provider, with a ten-minute paste window. The cache is cleared on replacement, expiry while running, or explicit disable/forget. After an interrupted process, cleanup happens on next startup or access. An app you paste into may retain its own copy.

Content marked sensitive is excluded, but not every app marks sensitive content. Do not assume all passwords or private text will be recognized automatically.

The optional keyboard.

AirBridge processes key presses and the current word on your phone to enter text in the app you are using and offer English word suggestions. It does not save a typing history or send typing to the developer or your Mac. Suggestions use a bundled dictionary, not a cloud typing service. Keyboard appearance and height are saved locally. Emoji recents are limited to the current picker session; emoji search queries are processed locally and are not saved as search history.

Saved phrases are text you explicitly enter in keyboard settings. Up to twenty phrases are stored in the app's private settings until you edit or remove them. Tapping a phrase or clipboard item inserts it into the app you are using, which may save or send that text according to its own practices. Do not store passwords in saved phrases.

Automatic clipboard sharing needs separate consent as well as selecting AirBridge as your keyboard. It can then send newly copied text and readable PNG/JPEG images to your paired Mac while other apps are open or the keyboard is hidden. Sharing pauses while the phone is locked or the field is marked private or as a password. Selecting the keyboard alone does not enable sharing. Switch keyboards or turn off automatic clipboard sync to stop this access.

Autofill and verification-code suggestions come from your selected Android autofill provider when supported. AirBridge hosts the provider's protected suggestions; it does not read your SMS inbox, email, saved passwords, or the protected suggestion contents. The provider and the app receiving the filled value have their own data-handling practices.

Voice typing and your speech provider.

Voice typing is optional. It requires Android microphone permission and starts when you tap the microphone in the keyboard. AirBridge passes speech to Android's speech recognition service and inserts the returned text into the app you are using. Tap the microphone again to stop; recognition also stops when the keyboard is hidden or its input session finishes.

AirBridge uses Android's on-device recognizer when available. Otherwise it uses your phone's installed speech service and requests offline recognition, but that request does not guarantee offline processing. The provider may send audio to its servers. Its processing, retention, and deletion practices are governed by that provider's settings and privacy policy. Check your selected speech provider before using voice typing for confidential information.

AirBridge does not save audio recordings, maintain a dictation history, or send audio to the developer or your paired Mac. The app receiving the dictated text may retain it. You can revoke microphone access in Android Settings at any time.

Your notifications, by permission.

You choose the Android apps that can forward notifications. Message previews are off by default and hidden while the phone is locked. Android-protected notification content is not bypassed.

With notification access granted and forwarding enabled, Android delivers notification events to AirBridge while other apps are open. App names and, when previews are enabled, notification titles and messages from selected apps are sent to your paired Mac. Notification contents can include sensitive information. The app picker uses locally available app names and icons; it does not upload your installed-app list to the developer.

AirBridge stores no notification title/body history. Active notifications are held in memory, while app-selection preferences and a bounded catalog of observed app names persist locally. macOS may retain delivered alerts in Notification Center, especially after a forced app shutdown. Its lock-screen and preview settings also apply.

Replies stay in memory while being dispatched and are not saved by AirBridge. The receiving messaging app processes and retains replies according to its own behavior.

Screen and camera sharing.

Wi-Fi screen sharing requires a manual start and fresh Android approval for every session. AirBridge does not save frames or consent tokens, and does not record audio. It does not automatically restart capture after reconnecting. Protected content remains protected.

In builds with live camera sharing, you select the front or rear camera and approve a session on the phone. Camera permission allows AirBridge to stream that camera's video to your paired Mac over the local network. AirBridge buffers live video in memory without recording it or sharing phone audio. You can stop from the phone or Mac; capture also ends if the phone locks or the connection fails. A new session requires a new start and approval.

Screen and camera feeds can reveal anything visible in the shared screen or scene. If you share the Mac preview or use AirBridge's camera output in a video-call app where supported, that app and the call's participants may transmit, capture, or retain it under their own settings and policies.

What permissions are for.

  • Camera: scanning pairing QR codes and, when you approve it in a supported build, live camera sharing.
  • Microphone: voice typing through Android's speech recognizer after you tap the microphone.
  • Notification access: forwarding notifications from your selected apps and handling supported replies. Permission to show AirBridge's own notifications is separate and is used for transfer progress, incoming requests, and active connections or sharing sessions.
  • Screen capture: sharing the screen or app content you approve in Android's capture prompt for that session.
  • Keyboard access: entering text, displaying suggestions, and using clipboard features as described above. AirBridge does not use an accessibility service to monitor input.
  • Files and package installation: accessing files you choose, saving received files, and opening selected APK files in Android's installer with your confirmation. AirBridge does not request access to your entire photo library.
  • Network and background operation: discovering and communicating with your paired Mac and keeping active transfers or enabled connection features running.

This website and support.

This version of the website includes no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising scripts, or account forms. Fonts and product images are served with the site. If you choose light or dark mode, that preference is saved in this browser's local storage.

Visiting any hosted website sends basic request information, including your IP address, to its hosting provider. Website hosting and release-download providers may keep access logs under their own policies. Download links, when available, take you to the release host.

This site and public downloads use GitHub hosting. See GitHub's privacy statement. If you email support, your email provider and ours process the message. We use the information you send to answer your request; please avoid sending passwords, pairing codes, or private files. This may include your email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve your request and follow up; you can request deletion using the contact address above, subject to any legally required retention.

Change your mind at any time.

Turn off individual features in either app. Revoke Android notification, camera, or microphone permissions through system settings. Remove a phone in the Mac app to revoke its pairing. Shared or received files are separate from these permissions; delete those files from their saved location when you no longer need them.

Remove saved phrases under Keyboard settings > Edit saved phrases and save your changes. Keyboard clipboard history exists only for the lifetime of its service; force-stopping AirBridge in Android Settings ends that process and clears this in-memory history. Clearing the app's storage removes its private preferences, saved phrases, credentials, and cache. AirBridge disables Android backup of its private app data.

Uninstalling Android AirBridge removes its private settings, credentials, and cached data, but not files saved in shared Downloads or data retained by another app or device. Remove the pairing and received files on each device separately. We cannot remotely erase data on your devices because AirBridge has no cloud account or central copy. Contact us to request deletion of information you sent directly to support; limited records may need to be retained where legally required.

Retention depends on the data: received files remain until deleted, saved settings and phrases remain until removed, and temporary clipboard, notification, and video data follow the limits described above. AirBridge does not create user accounts, so there is no separate AirBridge account to delete. See the setup guide for feature-specific controls and limitations.

Changes to this policy.

We will update this page when AirBridge's data handling changes and revise the date at the top. New permissions or optional features may also require additional notices or consent in the app.
